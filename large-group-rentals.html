GROUPS OF 8 OR MORE

Large group vacation rentals in Kentucky

Family reunions, friend trips and Bourbon Trail weekends: our homes for 8 to 14 guests, plus three Gorge cabins that sleep 19 together.

THE AREA

Why stay here

Planning a family reunion, a friends' trip to the Bourbon Trail or a big weekend in the Red River Gorge? These are our homes built for groups of 8 or more. Each has room to gather and enough beds and baths to go around.

Bourbon Street is the biggest: a 1904 mansion in downtown Frankfort with 7 bedrooms, a speakeasy bar, billiards, a poker room and a theater. Herrington Haven puts 10 people on the lake with a private dock. Bootlegger's Retreat and The Woodleigh each sleep 8.

Need room for more than 14? Our three Red River Gorge cabins, Bootlegger's Retreat, Trinity A-Frame and NEO, are in the same hollow and sleep 19 together. Call or text 859-229-1524 and we'll help you line up the dates.

Good to know for groups

No parties or events

Our homes are for groups staying together, not parties or events.

Stairs at Bourbon Street

All bedrooms and full baths are upstairs.

Gorge driving

AWD or 4WD is recommended for the cabins, and required in ice or snow.

Pets

Only the three Red River Gorge cabins are pet friendly.

Questions travelers ask

What is the largest home?

Bourbon Street in downtown Frankfort: 7 bedrooms and 3.5 baths for up to 14 guests.

Where can a large family stay near Buffalo Trace?

Bourbon Street sleeps 14 and is about 5 minutes from Buffalo Trace, in walkable downtown Frankfort.

Where can a big group stay in the Red River Gorge?

Book our three cabins in Birch Hollow together for up to 19 guests, or Bootlegger's Retreat alone for up to 8.

Are parties or events allowed?

No. Our homes are not available for parties or events.

Can I book more than one home directly?

Yes. Book each home on its own page, or call or text 859-229-1524 and we will help you coordinate dates.

Can I book directly instead of through Airbnb or Vrbo?

Yes. Every home on this site has a live calendar and secure checkout, so you can book directly with us. Booking here is always cheaper than the same stay on Airbnb or Vrbo, and direct guests get a complimentary early check-in or late checkout of up to 2 hours when available.
