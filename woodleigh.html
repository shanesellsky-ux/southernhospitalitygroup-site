Good to know

How does check-in work?

Self check-in with a smart lock. Check-in is 3:00 pm.

What do I need to do at checkout?

Lock up, turn off the lights and log out of any streaming accounts. Checkout is 11:00 am.

Where do we park?

The driveway fits 4 cars. Please do not park at the veterinary clinic across the street.

Are pets allowed?

No pets, please. If you have a service animal, contact us before arrival.

How far is Keeneland?

About 15 minutes, and Lexington is about 15 minutes away. Bourbon Trail distilleries are minutes from the house.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a full refund. Cancel 7 to 30 days before check-in and 50% of the nightly total is refunded. Cancellations less than 7 days before check-in are non-refundable.
