THE AREA

Why stay here

The Red River Gorge is in the Daniel Boone National Forest in eastern Kentucky, known for its natural sandstone arches, high cliffs and some of the best sport climbing in the country. Natural Bridge State Resort Park sits just south of Slade, and Miguel's Pizza is the climbers' base camp.

Our three cabins, Bootlegger's Retreat, Trinity A-Frame and NEO, are in Birch Hollow between Stanton and Slade, about 10 minutes from the Gorge. Every one has a private hot tub, a fire pit, a free Level 2 EV charger and room for up to two pets.

Traveling with a bigger crew? All three cabins are in Birch Hollow and sleep 19 together (8 + 6 + 5). Book each on its page, or call or text 859-229-1524 and we'll help you line up the dates.

Questions travelers ask

Where should a group stay when visiting the Red River Gorge?

Bootlegger's Retreat sleeps 8 across 4 bedrooms and has a game room for downtime. For a bigger group, book all three of our cabins: they're in the same hollow between Stanton and Slade and sleep 19 together.

Are there family cabins near Natural Bridge State Resort Park?

Yes. All three cabins are about 10 minutes from Slade, with Natural Bridge a short drive beyond. Trinity A-Frame (3 bedrooms, sleeps 6) and Bootlegger's Retreat (4 bedrooms, sleeps 8, with a gated kids' loft) work well for families.

Do the Red River Gorge cabins have hot tubs?

Yes. Every cabin has its own private hot tub on the deck. Our Herrington Lake house has one too.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. All three Gorge cabins welcome up to 2 pets for $50 per pet per stay.

Do I need 4WD to reach the cabins?

The cabins sit up a gravel road in Birch Hollow. AWD or 4WD is recommended in good weather and required in ice or snow.

Is there cell service?

Cell signal can be spotty in the Gorge. Every cabin has Wi-Fi, so turn on Wi-Fi calling.

Can I book directly instead of through Airbnb or Vrbo?

Yes. Every home on this site has a live calendar and secure checkout, so you can book directly with us. Booking here is always cheaper than the same stay on Airbnb or Vrbo, and direct guests get a complimentary early check-in or late checkout of up to 2 hours when available.
