Three cabins in the woods about 10 minutes from Slade. Every one has a hot tub, a fire pit and free EV charging. Climb, hike and paddle all day, then soak.

THE AREA

Why stay here.

The Gorge is Kentucky at its wildest: sandstone arches, cliffs for climbing, the Red River for paddling and Natural Bridge just down the road. Our three cabins sit in the woods close to Slade and Miguel's, with hot tubs on every deck and game rooms for rainy days.
