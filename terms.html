TERMS OF STAY

The fine print, in plain words.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Booking and payment

These terms apply to stays booked directly on southernhospitalitygroup.co. Stays booked through Airbnb or Vrbo follow that platform's terms and the policies shown on its listing.

Reservations and payments are processed securely through Hospitable. The total shown at checkout, including the nightly rate, cleaning fee, service fee and taxes, is the amount you pay. By completing a booking you agree to these terms and to the house rules for your home.

The person who books is responsible for everyone in their group and for following these terms and the house rules.

Cancellations

Cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a full refund.

Cancel 7 to 30 days before check-in and 50% of the nightly total is refunded.

Cancellations less than 7 days before check-in are non-refundable.

We strongly recommend travel insurance. Kentucky weather can change quickly, and we can't offer refunds for weather, road conditions or travel delays outside our control.

Changes

To change your dates or number of guests, message us or call or text 859-229-1524. Changes depend on availability and may change the price.

Check-in and checkout

Check-in is 3:00 pm. Checkout is 10:00 am, except The Woodleigh, which checks out at 11:00 am.

Early check-in or late checkout depends on the calendar. Direct bookings get a complimentary early check-in or late checkout of up to 2 hours when available; anything beyond that must be agreed with us in advance.

Check-in is contactless. Your door code and arrival directions are sent before your stay. Please don't share your code with anyone outside your group.

Length of stay and occupancy

The maximum stay is 28 nights.

The number of guests may not exceed the maximum occupancy listed for your home, and only registered guests may stay overnight. Visitors beyond your registered group need our approval in advance.

House rules

No parties or events.

No smoking or vaping inside any home. You're welcome to smoke outside on a deck or porch; please dispose of butts properly. Evidence of smoking inside may result in a cleaning fee (for example, $300 at the Trinity A-Frame).

Please be respectful of neighbors, and leave furniture where you found it.

Follow any posted rules for hot tubs, fire pits, docks, game rooms and other amenities.

Pets

Pets are welcome only at our three Red River Gorge cabins (Bootlegger's Retreat, NEO and Trinity A-Frame): up to 2 pets, $50 per pet per stay. Please include pets in your booking.

Pets must be crated if left alone inside, kept off the furniture and never left unattended outside. Owners are responsible for any damage caused by their pets.

Our Bourbon Trail homes and Herrington Haven are pet free.

Damage and responsibility

You are responsible for any damage to the home or its contents caused by you, your guests or your pets beyond normal wear and tear, and for any extra cleaning needed beyond a normal turnover. Please tell us right away if something breaks; accidents happen and we'd rather know.

Amenities such as hot tubs, fire pits, grills, docks, stairs and game equipment are used at your own risk. Please supervise children at all times. Some homes have steep driveways, gravel roads, ladders or steep steps; these are noted on each home's page.

We're not responsible for lost or stolen belongings, or for interruptions to utilities, Wi-Fi, cell service or streaming services outside our control. We'll always do our best to fix any problem quickly.

Access and maintenance

We or our team may need to enter the home during your stay for urgent maintenance or safety reasons. Whenever possible, we'll contact you first.

Contact

Questions about these terms or your booking? Call or text Shane at 859-229-1524.
