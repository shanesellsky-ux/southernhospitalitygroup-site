Good to know

How does check-in work?

Self check-in with a smart lock. Check-in is 3:00 pm.

What do I need to do at checkout?

Grab your things, lock the doors and drop trash in the bear box by the driveway. Starting the dishwasher is appreciated but optional. Checkout is 10:00 am.

Is the hot tub private?

Yes. The sunken hot tub on the covered back deck is private.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Up to 2 pets for $50 per pet per stay. Pets must be crated if left alone inside.

What is there to do inside?

A light-up shuffleboard table, a retro multicade with thousands of games, card games and Starlink Wi-Fi.

What vehicle do I need?

AWD or 4WD is recommended in good weather and required in ice or snow. Most AWD cars, Teslas included, handle it fine. There’s one steeper hill near the top, so just keep your momentum. The driveway fits 2 cars.

Is firewood provided?

The fire pit is wood-burning, and there’s some firewood shared between Trinity and NEO to get you started. Need more? The firewood shed where the blacktop ends sells bundles by cash box or Venmo.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a full refund. Cancel 7 to 30 days before check-in and 50% of the nightly total is refunded. Cancellations less than 7 days before check-in are non-refundable.
