Read the FAQ

Can I book these homes directly?

Yes. Every home on this site has a live calendar and secure checkout. Booking direct is always cheaper than the same stay on Airbnb or Vrbo.

What are the benefits of booking directly with Southern Hospitality Group?

Our best price, a complimentary early check-in or late checkout of up to 2 hours when available, and a direct line to your local hosts instead of a call center.

Is booking direct secure?

Yes. Reservations and payments on this site run through Hospitable, the same system that manages our calendars on Airbnb and Vrbo, with secure online payment.

Is check-in contactless?

Yes. Every home has self check-in with a keypad or smart lock. You get a personal door code and arrival directions before your stay, so there is no key pickup and no waiting on anyone.

What time is check-in and checkout?

Check-in is 3:00 pm at every home. Checkout is 10:00 am, except The Woodleigh, which checks out at 11:00 am.

Do you have a long list of checkout chores?

No. Checkout is simple: lock the doors, turn off the lights, log out of any personal streaming accounts and, at the cabins and lake house, drop the trash in the bins by the driveway. You do not need to strip beds or do laundry. We take care of that.
