Good to know

How does check-in work?

Self check-in with a smart lock. Your personal door code is ready at 3:00 pm.

What do I need to do at checkout?

Just lock both doors on your way out. Checkout is 10:00 am.

Is the hot tub private?

Yes. The hot tub on the deck is private to NEO.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Up to 2 pets for $50 per pet per stay. A dog bed and bowls are provided. Pets must be crated if left alone inside.

How many people does it sleep?

Up to 5: a king loft, a queen bedroom and extra linens for a fifth guest.

What vehicle do I need?

AWD or 4WD is recommended in good weather and required in ice or snow. Most AWD cars, Teslas included, handle it fine. There’s one steeper hill near the top, so just keep your momentum. The driveway fits 2 cars.

Is firewood provided?

The fire pit is wood-burning, and there’s some firewood shared between NEO and the Trinity A-Frame to get you started. Need more? The firewood shed where the blacktop ends sells bundles by cash box or Venmo.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a full refund. Cancel 7 to 30 days before check-in and 50% of the nightly total is refunded. Cancellations less than 7 days before check-in are non-refundable.
