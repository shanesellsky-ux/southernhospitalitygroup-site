FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good to know.

Short answers to the questions guests ask most. Each home's page has its own details too.

Still have a question? Call or text 859-229-1524.

Is check-in contactless?

Yes. Every home has self check-in with a keypad or smart lock. You get a personal door code and arrival directions before your stay, so there is no key pickup and no waiting on anyone.

What time is check-in and checkout?

Check-in is 3:00 pm and checkout is 10:00 am at most homes. Trinity A-Frame checks in at 4:00 pm, and The Woodleigh checks out at 11:00 am.

Do you have a long list of checkout chores?

No. Checkout is simple: lock the doors, turn off the lights, log out of any personal streaming accounts and, at the cabins and lake house, drop the trash in the bins by the driveway. You do not need to strip beds or do laundry. We take care of that.

What do I get for booking direct?

A complimentary early check-in or late checkout of up to 2 hours, when the calendar allows. You also skip the booking-site service fee and deal directly with us.

Which homes are pet friendly?

Our three Red River Gorge cabins: Bootlegger's Retreat, NEO and Trinity A-Frame. Our Bourbon Trail and Herrington Lake homes are pet free.

What are the pet rules?

The pet fee is $50 per pet per stay, with a maximum of 2 pets. Please include pets in your booking. Pets cannot be left unattended outside, and must be crated if left alone inside. Owners are responsible for any damage caused by their pets.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a full refund. Cancel 7 to 30 days before check-in and 50% of the nightly total is refunded. Cancellations less than 7 days before check-in are non-refundable.

Do I need 4WD to reach the Red River Gorge cabins?

The cabins sit up a gravel road in Birch Hollow. AWD or 4WD is recommended in good weather and required in ice or snow. Rear-wheel-drive cars are not recommended.

Is there Wi-Fi and cell service?

Every home has Wi-Fi. Cell signal can be spotty in the Red River Gorge, so turning on Wi-Fi calling on your phone helps.

Can I charge an electric car?

Yes. All three Red River Gorge cabins have a free Level 2 EV charger.

Can I smoke at the property?

Not indoors. Every home has a strict no-smoking rule inside. You are welcome to smoke outside on a deck or porch; please dispose of butts properly.

Are parties or events allowed?

No. Our homes are not available for parties or events.

How do I reach you during my stay?

Message us any time, or call or text 859-229-1524. We are local and answer quickly.
